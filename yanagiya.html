About Yanagiya

Yanagiya is a masterfully preserved Japanese merchant townhouse located in the heart of Magome-juku. When you book Yanagiya, you receive 100% private and exclusive access to the entire building — no sharing with strangers.

Traditional features including soaring dark-timber cedar beams, shoji lattice screens, and fresh igusa tatami matting create a timeless atmosphere, while modern touches like air conditioning/heating, high-speed Wi-Fi, modern shower and bath facilities, and a fully equipped kitchen ensure maximum relaxation.

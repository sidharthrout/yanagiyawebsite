Your Journey Here 🚂

A hand-crafted guide to reaching the Nakasendo

🐱

Konnichiwa! Welcome to our mountain! 🐾

Traveling into the Japanese Alps might seem a bit tricky at first glance, but it is actually one of the most gorgeous, scenic train rides in the country.

I've put together this simple step-by-step guide to help you find your way to our front door from Japan's biggest cities. Grab a bento box for the train, and let's go!

Approx 3.5 Hours

🗼 From Tokyo

🚅
Step 1: Shinkansen to Nagoya
Board the Tokaido Shinkansen (Bullet Train) from Tokyo Station or Shinagawa Station, heading west to Nagoya Station. (It takes about 1h 40m on the Nozomi).
🚂
Step 2: JR Shinano to Nakatsugawa
At Nagoya Station, transfer to the JR Shinano Limited Express. This train winds through a stunning river valley. Sit on the left side of the train for the best views! Ride it for about 50 minutes until you reach Nakatsugawa Station.
🚌
Step 3: Kita-Ena Bus to Magome
Exit Nakatsugawa Station and you will see the bus stops right in front. Look for the "Kita-Ena" bus bound for Magome (Platform 3). Ride it to the very last stop (about 25 mins). From the Magome bus stop, Yanagiya is just a beautiful short walk up the stone path!
Approx 2.5 Hours

🐙 From Kyoto or Osaka

🚅
Step 1: Shinkansen to Nagoya
Board the Tokaido Shinkansen heading east (towards Tokyo). Get off at Nagoya Station. (Only 35 minutes from Kyoto, or 50 minutes from Shin-Osaka).
🚂
Step 2: JR Shinano to Nakatsugawa
Just like the Tokyo route, transfer at Nagoya to the JR Shinano Limited Express and enjoy the 50-minute valley ride to Nakatsugawa Station.
🚌
Step 3: Bus to Magome
Hop on the local bus outside Nakatsugawa Station heading straight to Magome!
🧳

Lucky Cat's Pro Tip: Luggage Forwarding

Don't want to drag heavy suitcases on trains and buses? Use a "Ta-Q-Bin" (Yamato Transport) service from your hotel in Tokyo or Osaka! They will deliver your heavy luggage directly to Yanagiya the next day, so you can travel light and fully enjoy the Nakasendo hike!

Our Heritage

A Historic Machiya, Lovingly Reborn

Yanagiya began as a traditional merchant home along the ancient Nakasendo highway connecting Edo (Tokyo) and Kyoto. Restored with deep reverence for Japanese carpentry, our private lodges in Magome preserve cedar beams, shoji paper doors, and scenic engawa verandas — while providing modern comfort for international travelers.

Magome Cha

The People of Yanagiya

Meet Our Team

Our passionate local hosts, guides, and artisans dedicated to making your stay in Magome unforgettable. All of our team members speak fluent Japanese and English.

Founder & Host Japanese / English

Kenta Oba

Founder / Host

Based in Magome, Gifu, Kenta runs guesthouses, develops a local tea brand, and creates experiences such as hiking and stargazing that connect travelers with the nature, history, and culture of the area.

Before starting his own business, he worked for the local prefectural government. He later chose a different path — one that would allow him to work more closely with people, travel, and the local community.

Travel has always been an important part of his life. He has backpacked around the world, met people from many different cultures, and even traveled down the Amazon River on a handmade raft.

Through those experiences, he came to realize that the most memorable part of a journey is often not the famous sightseeing spot itself, but the people you meet, the local way of life, and the small experiences that give a place its character.

Today, his aim is to share a deeper side of Magome through places to stay, local tea and food, walking experiences, and the surrounding nature. As someone who lives and works here, Kenta hopes to help travelers discover not only Magome as a destination, but Magome as a place to experience, remember, and feel connected to.

"Discover Magome as a place to experience, remember, and feel connected to."

Tea & Local Guide Japanese / English

Riho

Tea & Local Guide

Riho has traveled around the world as a backpacker and fell in love with Magome along the way.

Today, she is involved in developing our tea business and working as a local tour guide, while also preparing to open her own guesthouse in Magome.

Fluent in English, she loves meeting and connecting with travelers from around the world.

She looks forward to welcoming you to Magome and sharing what makes this place so special.

"Excited to share the deep aroma of Magome Cha with travelers worldwide."

Food, Wine & Host Japanese / English / French

Yuji

Host, Food & Wine

Yuji lives locally and is also involved in wild boar hunting in the mountains around Magome.

He prepares evening meals at our guesthouse and welcomes guests as the host and caretaker of Yanagiya Iri.

Speaking Japanese, English, and French, Yuji loves meeting people from around the world. Guests can also enjoy seasonal dishes made with fresh river fish caught in the beautiful local streams.

Yuji is also a qualified sommelier and looks forward to introducing guests to the character and variety of Japanese wines. Through good food, wine, and conversation, he hopes to make your evenings in Magome especially memorable.

"Connecting travelers through mountain culinary traditions and fine Japanese wine."

Stargazing Guide Japanese / English

Eli

Stargazing & Local Guide

Eli has traveled around the world and studied abroad, and today she is involved in our stargazing tours and tea project.

Fluent in English, she loves meeting people, animals, and spending time in nature.

Drawn to the landscapes, people, and community of Magome, she is currently spending part of her life living here and experiencing the area more deeply.

She looks forward to meeting you and sharing the beauty of Magome through our tours.

"Sharing the quiet night wonder and starry skies of the Kiso mountains."

About Yanagiya Iri

Yanagiya Iri is nestled deeper into the slopes of Magome, surrounded by century-old Japanese cedar trees and ancient stone rice terraces. Designed for travelers seeking profound peace, silence, and communion with nature.

Sit out on the wooden engawa veranda listening to the mountain stream, sip handcrafted Magome Cha, or unwind in the private bath overlooking the forest.

🥂 Hosted Evening Dining by Yuji (Sommelier & Hunter)

Yanagiya Iri guests can enjoy exclusive seasonal botan nabe hot-pot dinners prepared by resident host Yuji. Featuring fresh mountain river fish, locally sourced wild boar game, and Japanese wine pairings curated by Yuji as a qualified sommelier.
